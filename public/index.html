Why it doesn't hallucinate

Grounding is trained in, not bolted on afterward with a separate checking step.

1

Trained on real evidence

Every training example follows a Chain-of-Evidence: each claim traces back to a verifiable source, not just plausible-sounding text.

2

Tuned to reason, not guess

Direct Preference Optimization specifically targets the reasoning errors that generic LLM judges miss — scope mismatches, category errors, misapplied facts.

3

Independently verified

Every answer in our benchmark was checked claim-by-claim against its source document, by hand — not a single automated pass/fail score.
