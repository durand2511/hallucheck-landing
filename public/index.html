How it works

Built on the Chain-of-Evidence method, with a fine-tuned grounding model trained specifically to catch reasoning errors that generic LLM judges miss.

1

Send an answer

Your app calls /v1/verify with the model's answer — sources are optional, HalluCheck can fetch its own evidence.

2

Claim-by-claim verification

Every checkable claim is extracted and matched against evidence — citation checks, numeric checks, and entailment against the source.

3

Get a verdict

You get back a score plus a per-claim breakdown — supported, contradicted, or unverifiable — so you know exactly what to trust.
